English Tester Guide

This app is currently Korean-first. Please check the core flow below.

  1. Open Today Matches and select a match.
  2. Try an odds search and open the match details.
  3. Sign in with Google and add or remove a favorite.
  4. Check navigation, loading, text clipping, and crashes.

Keep the app installed and remain opted in for at least 14 days.
